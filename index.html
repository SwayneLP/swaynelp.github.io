Personnal (Theorical) Project

Designing for designers

Spark is a stationery brand made by and for designers, inspired by metal engineering and timeless industrial design. The goal was to create a brand that feels reliable and precise, with a premium look and high-quality materials. Creation of a wordmark and brand system, including 3D renders and blueprint-style illustrations, applied across posters, billboards, and motion design.

Personnal (Theorical) Project

Run, meet and have fun !

NRV RUN is a running social club from a small city, where people can share their passion for running while meeting people from very different backgrounds. The runs take place through the town, fields, and forests, creating a variety of landscapes and encounters. Creation of an icon and a wordmark based on one of my typefaces, “Baton”. The colors are inspired by nature and the paths where the runs take place.

Personnal (Theorical) Project

GOOD Artist always stays long term

Alpha Wann is one of my favorite rappers, so I imagined a new album art direction based on one of his recurring quotes: “Le rap c’est un marathon” (“Rap is a marathon”). The idea is that, like marathon runners, the greatest artists endure by creating great work throughout their lives. Creation of a typeface for the album title and tracklist. The tracklist is inspired by runners on an athletics track, while the back cover follows the route of the Paris Marathon.

Personnal (Theorical) Project

We're already in a cyberpunk era

I imagined what a major tech company like Sony could look like in the future. Inspired by Sony’s futuristic and provocative advertising from the early 2000s, this fictional version of Sony no longer produces phones, televisions, or speakers, but high-tech, mainstream human implants for people who want to challenge social norms. The wordmark uses cut lines and curves to represent replaceable parts of the human body. The “O” also contains an Easter egg referencing the PlayStation controller buttons. The color palette is inspired by the neon lights of Tokyo at night.

Client Project

Party is a neural system

Can’t Stop is an event company that organizes silent parties. The challenge was to create a symbol of unity despite everyone being separated by their headphones. The icon is inspired by neural connections: each person is alone with their headphones, yet remains an essential part of the collective party system. It also represents a sun shining through the night, evoking the joy and energy of the party.

Personnal (Theorical) Project

Modular makes music touchable again

Klei Modular is a modular music brand focused on the tactile experience of making music. The logo is based on a modular grid that allows its composition to change and evolve. The “KLEI” lettering has a rough, imperfect finish to preserve a handmade feeling.

Baton

Personnal Project

JALLEAU

This typeface was created in memory of Franck Jalleau, the person who completely immersed me in the world of typography. Inspired by lapidary engraving, it refers to the first workshop I attended with him during my studies. For me, this technique best represents his approach to typography and his influence on my work.

Personnal Project

ARUTEXT

An anti-textura typeface in which the traditional contrast is inverted. An experiment exploring how far the hand-drawn appearance of early printed typefaces can be deconstructed.

Personnal Project

SAINT TROP

Inspired by sign painting and vintage posters from the South of France, Saint Trop is an all-caps serif typeface. It is a tribute to good vibes, sunshine, and the slow-life spirit of the Mediterranean.

Personnal Project

Hand Type Program

Initially created for a school project, this interactive typeface was designed using only a mouse. I wanted to create something special for my school’s open day. The program tracks the user’s index finger and thumb, allowing them to interactively modify the shape of the circles that make up the typeface.
